# Jalia Healthcare

> **One place to understand the journey with endometriosis.**

Jalia Healthcare is a digital health platform focused on helping people understand, recognize, navigate, and live with endometriosis.

Jalia is designed around the **endometriosis journey**, beginning before a person even knows the word "endometriosis."

A person may start with:

> "Why are my periods this painful?"

Jalia helps them move toward:

> "Could this be more than ordinary period pain?"

Then:

> "What is endometriosis?"

Then:

> "How can I understand what I'm experiencing?"

Then:

> "How do I get appropriate help?"

And eventually:

> "I understand my condition. How do I navigate and manage life with it?"

Jalia combines education, multimedia learning, personal health tracking, healthcare preparation, journey history, and practical support into one experience.

# About Jalia

Jalia Healthcare is an endometriosis-focused digital health product designed to make the endometriosis journey easier to understand and navigate.

Endometriosis can affect many areas of a person's life, yet people may spend years trying to understand why they experience severe or recurring pain.

Jalia is designed to meet users at different points in that journey.

Someone might arrive because:

- their periods are extremely painful
- pain is interfering with school or work
- pain medication does not seem to help enough
- they experience pelvic pain outside their period
- they experience bowel or urinary symptoms
- they experience pain during sex
- their symptoms are becoming worse
- they are tired of being told that severe pain is simply "normal"
- they have started wondering whether their symptoms could be related to endometriosis
- they have already been diagnosed and want help understanding the condition

Jalia does not assume that every user knows what endometriosis is.

That is intentional.

The product starts with the person's **experience**, not the medical label.

---

# The Problem

Severe menstrual and pelvic pain can be normalized for years.

People may hear:

> "Periods are supposed to hurt."

or:

> "It's normal."

or:

> "You'll grow out of it."

This can make it difficult to recognize when recurring or severe symptoms deserve further attention.

At the same time, information about endometriosis is often:

- fragmented
- overly clinical
- difficult to understand
- primarily available in English
- disconnected from a person's real-life journey
- scattered across websites, social media, videos, and healthcare resources

Even after discovering endometriosis, users may still have questions:

- What exactly is it?
- Could my symptoms be related?
- What should I do next?
- Who should I talk to?
- What happens during diagnosis?
- What questions should I ask?
- What does treatment involve?
- What does living with endometriosis look like?

Jalia is designed around these questions.

# The Vision

Jalia's vision is to become a trusted digital companion for the endometriosis journey.

The long-term vision is not simply to provide more health information.

It is to help users:

**Recognize → Understand → Seek Help → Navigate → Manage → Continue**

Jalia aims to make endometriosis information:

- understandable
- accessible
- visual
- culturally relevant
- bilingual
- practical
- trustworthy
- available even with limited connectivity

---

# What Jalia Is

Jalia is an:

### Endometriosis education platform

Helping users understand endometriosis through articles, illustrations, videos, audio, interactive experiences, FAQs, and stories.

### Journey companion

Helping users understand where they are in their personal endometriosis journey and what they may want to explore next.

### Personal health organization tool

Helping users record their periods, symptoms, pain, daily-life impact, appointments, treatment information, and important journey events.

### Healthcare preparation tool

Helping users organize information and prepare questions before speaking with healthcare professionals.

### Bilingual health experience

Providing meaningful educational experiences in both English and Kiswahili.

### Low-connectivity digital health product

Designed with offline functionality and limited-data environments in mind.

---

# What Jalia Is Not

Jalia is deliberately **not**:

- a diagnostic tool
- an AI doctor
- a replacement for healthcare professionals
- a telemedicine service
- a hospital booking marketplace
- a pharmacy
- an insurance platform
- a generic period tracker
- a generic women's health app
- a social media platform

Jalia can help a person recognize that their experiences may deserve attention.

It must not tell a person:

> "You have endometriosis."

# The Endometriosis Journey

The core of Jalia is the journey.

The experience is organized around several stages.

## 1. Something Doesn't Feel Normal

The person may not know anything about endometriosis yet.

They may simply be experiencing:

- severe period pain
- worsening pain
- pelvic pain
- pain outside menstruation
- bowel-related symptoms
- urinary symptoms
- pain during sex
- heavy bleeding
- fatigue
- disrupted sleep
- missed school
- missed work
- difficulty participating in normal activities

Jalia helps the user understand that severe symptoms do not automatically have to be dismissed as "just period pain."

---

## 2. Could This Be Endometriosis?

Jalia introduces the concept of endometriosis carefully.

The user can explore questions such as:

- Could severe period pain be something more?
- What symptoms can occur with endometriosis?
- What makes recurring pain worth discussing?
- What symptoms can happen outside menstruation?

The experience is educational, not diagnostic.

The product clearly communicates that symptoms can have different causes and that a healthcare professional is needed for proper assessment.

---

## 3. Understand Endometriosis

The user can progressively learn:

- what endometriosis is
- what happens in the body
- possible symptoms
- where endometriosis can occur
- how symptoms may affect daily life
- how endometriosis can differ from ordinary menstrual cramps
- other conditions that can cause similar symptoms
- diagnosis
- treatment
- living with endometriosis

Content is delivered through multiple learning formats.

---

## 4. Understand My Experience

Tracking becomes useful here.

Users can record:

- period dates
- pain
- pain location
- pain severity
- symptoms
- symptoms outside menstruation
- bowel symptoms
- urinary symptoms
- fatigue
- bleeding
- medication and perceived effect
- sleep disruption
- impact on school
- impact on work
- impact on exercise
- impact on relationships
- other daily-life effects

Jalia uses this information to help users organize and understand their experiences.

It does not use tracking data to diagnose endometriosis.

---

## 5. Get Help

Jalia helps users understand how to seek appropriate healthcare support.

This may include:

- when symptoms deserve attention
- how to describe symptoms
- what information to bring
- questions to ask
- what may happen during an appointment
- what diagnosis can involve
- what different investigations may mean
- how to advocate for clarification
- when another professional opinion may be appropriate

---

## 6. Diagnosis

For users who are navigating diagnosis, Jalia can provide education about:

- what diagnosis means
- why diagnosis can take time
- what healthcare professionals may ask
- possible examinations
- possible investigations
- questions to discuss with healthcare professionals

Jalia does not determine whether the user has endometriosis.

---

## 7. Treatment

For users who have reached the treatment stage, Jalia can explain treatment categories and help users prepare for conversations with healthcare professionals.

Educational topics may include:

- pain management
- hormonal treatments
- surgery
- follow-up care
- treatment changes
- treatment side effects

The product does not prescribe treatment.

---

## 8. Living With Endometriosis

Endometriosis can affect more than periods.

Jalia can provide practical education around:

- school
- university
- work
- relationships
- sexual health
- fertility
- fatigue
- emotional wellbeing
- exercise
- flare-ups
- treatment follow-up

The goal is not to define a person by their condition.

Endometriosis is part of someone's health journey, not their entire identity.
